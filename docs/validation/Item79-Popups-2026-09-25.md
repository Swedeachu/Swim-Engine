### New cases (13)

- **`UI.Selection` (6):**
  - Radio groups: pointer selection focuses the group, wrapping arrows, Home/End, values set from code, read-only groups, registration rules.
  - List views: reveal while stepping, page sizes taken from the viewport, End/Home, `Submit`; the list's scroll bar keeps focus on the list.
  - Dropdowns: open below the dropdown at least as wide; highlight versus selection, commit by Enter or click; the label follows the choice; closed-state keys; Escape; light dismiss that still delivers the click; Tab closes the list; popup validation.
  - Long dropdown lists: capped height, and the selection is revealed on open and on End.
  - Virtual lists: 10,000 items shown with about 12 rows; rebinding far down the list; click and End selection on rows that are not bound; `SetItemCount` clamping; the length limit.
  - Editable slider values: Enter, blur, clamping and snapping, unparsable text; typing is not overwritten by value changes made from code.
- **`UI.Popups` (5):**
  - Placement: below the anchor, flipping, clamping, Right/AtPoint/Center, DPI.
  - Paint and hit order; close, and removing an open popup.
  - Menus: focus-first; arrows confined to the menu; submenus; Escape and the stack order; activation from keys and pointer (the menu background does not activate); light dismiss and sticky popups; unrelated popups close when another opens.
  - Context menus: at the pointer (registered on an ancestor) and below the focused node.
  - Tooltips: delay, suppression after a press, targets that are not interactive, leaving, Escape, unregistering.
  - Modal dialogs: centered over a full scrim; the page gets no hits, clicks, Tab or wheel; Escape restores focus; buttons do not close the dialog on their own.
- **`UiInput.Bridge.RightClickShiftF10AndGamepadNorthOpenContextMenus`:** the three ways of opening a context menu, plus activation with gamepad South.

## Visual check

A scratch program laid out a settings panel with the theme and the fixture fonts, then drew it with the CPU reference (`Ui::Rasterize`, the renderer's definition). The panel had:

- a radio group;
- a dropdown;
- a slider with an editable value;
- a button with a tooltip;
- a list view;
- a File menu.

The program then opened, in turn, the dropdown, the File menu and the tooltip together, and a modal dialog. In the images:

- the dropdown list sits under its anchor at matching width, with the selection and highlight distinct;
- the menu opens to the right of its button, with a separator;
- the tooltip sits under the pointer;
- the modal is centered, and the scrim dims the page.

This check found two bugs:

- **Unrelated popups:** closing a dropdown also closed every popup opened after it, so opening a menu while a list was open lost the menu once focus moved. Opening a popup now first closes the light-dismiss popups above the one that holds its anchor. A submenu keeps its parent; an unrelated menu replaces an open list. `UI.Popups` covers both cases.
- **List view size:** themed list views lost the caller's size, because the theme's layout part replaced Width and Height. `ListView` now applies only the paint part of its theme class.
