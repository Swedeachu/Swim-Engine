**Clamping before the blur (rejected design):**

| Slices per pixel | Mean on the open floor |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.86 |
| 2 | 0.98 |

A single slice of an open surface can integrate above 1, so clamping each estimate biased open floors dark. Clamping after the 5×5 blur gives 0.996–0.998 for one to four slices. The raw GTAO output is therefore unclamped.

### Defects found

- **Rejected design — an analytic split-sum fit:** Karis' mobile fit was the first LUT-free source of the reflectance. Against this engine's height-correlated LUT it is off by up to 0.40 (grazing, rough), and a refit of its nine constants still left 0.27. The renderer now binds a LUT without an environment instead.
- **Test-only:** the first reflection tests expected every hit's travelled distance within 0.15 m and a short ray never to hit where a long one hit far away. Long rays take coarser steps (the step count is capped), so both are now statistical or bound checks.
- The clang check found a shadowed `dt` and an unneeded lambda capture in the item 77 code; both fixed.
