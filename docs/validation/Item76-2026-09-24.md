# Item 76 (first part) validation — September 24, 2026

This record covers the first part of critical-path item **76**: screen-space ambient occlusion and height fog.

- **Forward+ surface targets:**
  - `ForwardPlusTargets::Normal` and `ForwardPlusTargets::Indirect` (RGBA16Float);
  - five opaque and two transparent color attachments;
  - `ForwardPlus::IndirectRadiance`;
  - `ClusteredForward.slang`: `SV_Target3` and `SV_Target4`, and the transparent variant's transmittance output.
- **`Renderer/ScreenSpace`:** `ScreenSpaceSettings`, `ScreenSpaceRecords`, `ScreenSpaceBindings`, `ScreenSpaceReference`, `ScreenSpaceEffects` and `ScreenSpaceGraphResources`.
- **Shaders:** `ScreenSpaceRecords.slang`, `ScreenSpaceAo.slang`, `ScreenSpaceBlur.slang` and `ScreenSpaceComposite.slang` (`SwimScreenSpaceAo`, `SwimScreenSpaceBlur`, `SwimScreenSpaceComposite`).
- **Test fixture:** `Tests/Fixtures/ScreenSpaceFixture.h`, analytic ray-cast scenes with jitter.

Screen-space reflections, the rest of item 76, are not part of this checkpoint. The contract is in [Screen-space effects](../ScreenSpace.md). The engine runtime does not construct the module yet (item 56), and nothing legacy was retired.

### GTAO on the CPU

The measurements over the wall scene (160×90, radius 1 m, 2 slices, 4 steps, four noise frames, after the blur) were:

| Region | Mean visibility |
| --- | ---: |
| floor within 0.1 m of the wall | 0.62 |
| floor 0.4–0.6 m from the wall | 0.93 |
| open floor, 1.3–2.5 m away (beyond the radius) | 0.99 (minimum 0.98) |
| open wall | 0.99 |

**Clamping before the blur (rejected design):**

| Slices per pixel | Mean on the open floor |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.86 |
| 2 | 0.98 |

A single slice of an open surface can integrate above 1, so clamping each estimate biased open floors dark. Clamping after the 5×5 blur gives 0.996–0.998 for one to four slices. The raw GTAO output is therefore unclamped.

### CPU dry run of the native scene

The smoke's crease check was set from a CPU run of the same scene. The floor within 0.1 m of the wall (|z| < 6) averages 0.69 in the AO frame and 0.45 in the full frame, against 0.996 on the open floor. The smoke requires a gap of more than 0.2.

### Environment

The environment is the same as [item 44](Item44-2026-09-22.md). EnTT was supplied through `CMAKE_PROJECT_INCLUDE` in the sanitizer build directory only. mimalloc's override was disabled for that directory only, and `cmake/MemoryDependencies.cmake` was restored afterwards.

## Defects found

- **Slang intrinsic names:** locals named `distance`, `length`, `sign` and `step` shadowed Slang intrinsics. They are now `sampleDistance`, `pathLength`, `offsetSign` and `stepIndex`.
- **Biased open surfaces:** see "GTAO on the CPU" above.
- **Test-only:**
  - The first fog composite test ignored the max distance.
  - The first sun-lobe check looked along the light's travel instead of toward the sun.
  - The first crease band included floor far beyond the wall's ends.
