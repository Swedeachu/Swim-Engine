### Native smoke design

**`EnvironmentMapsMatchTheirCpuReferences`** builds three environments, each with a 64² source, a 32² × 5-mip prefiltered cube at 64 samples, 16² irradiance and a 32² LUT at 256 samples:

- the default sky;
- a white furnace of radiance 0.75;
- a brighter sky with the sun moved.

Every stage is compared with its CPU definition, fed the GPU's own read-back input:

- **Sky mip 0:** relative error < 3·10⁻³.
- **Each source mip:** equals the box filter of the mip above it (< 2·10⁻³).
- **Prefiltered cube:** `BuildPrefilteredCube` over the GPU source, with seamless trilinear emulation. Worst < 5 % and mean < 5·10⁻³.
- **SH coefficients:** < 0.5 % of the DC term. Evaluations at 64 random normals are within 1 %.
- **BRDF LUT:** < 3·10⁻³ absolute.
- **Furnace:** every prefiltered texel is 0.75 ± 10⁻³, and irradiance is 0.75 ± 5·10⁻³.

**`PbrGalleryMatchesTheCpuReference`** renders the 384×256 gallery with a 128² source, a 64² × 6-mip prefiltered cube at 128 samples and a 64² LUT at 512 samples, in three frames:

- sky + a sun-aligned light of radiance 3;
- image-based lighting only, intensity 0.6, rotated 1.1 rad;
- the furnace.

Checks:

- **Per pixel:** every pixel at least one pixel inside a silhouette is within 0.01 + 3 % of the CPU renderer. At most 0.1 % of pixels may be outliers, and the mean relative error must be < 4·10⁻³.
- **Background:** clear.
- **Alpha:** 1.
- **Furnace:** white dielectrics are 1 ± 1 % and nothing exceeds 1.01.
- **Rotated frame:** gold highlights dim monotonically with roughness.

`SWIM_PBR_GALLERY_DUMP=<dir>` writes the images.

**Tolerance headroom.** The tolerances were sized by re-running the CPU references with bilinear weights and lod fractions quantized to Vulkan's minimum 4-bit precision, and to the typical 8-bit precision:

| Comparison | 8-bit worst | 8-bit mean | 4-bit worst | 4-bit mean |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Prefilter | 0.1 % | 3·10⁻⁴ | 1.3 % | 2.9·10⁻³ |
| Gallery | 0.1 % | 4·10⁻⁵ | 1.7 % | 6·10⁻⁴ |

No gallery pixel left the tolerance in either case.

## Defects found

- **`uint3` std430 padding in three shader records (found by `ShaderCompiler.EnvironmentLayout`).** `uint Size; uint3 Reserved;` pads `Reserved` to offset 16, which made 32-byte push blocks instead of 16 and an 80-byte `GalleryView` instead of 64. The reserved fields are now scalars. The GPU would have read shifted constants.
- **Gallery pipeline creation failed on the first desktop run (RTX 4070, core validation).** `Rhi::DepthStencilState` defaults `DepthTest`/`DepthWrite` to true, and the Vulkan backend rejects depth state without a depth attachment. The gallery pipeline, which has no depth attachment, now disables both. On the same run, `EnvironmentMapsMatchTheirCpuReferences` passed with wide margins:
  - sky ≤ 9.7·10⁻⁴;
  - mips ≤ 8.5·10⁻⁴;
  - prefilter worst ≤ 2.6·10⁻³, mean 2.2·10⁻⁴;
  - LUT ≤ 4.9·10⁻⁴.

  The other 29 native cases and all 560 default cases also passed.
- **Dangling test references (found by AddressSanitizer).** `SWIM_CHECK_EQUAL(temporary()[0], …)` bound a reference into a temporary array. Those checks now compare by value.
