### Native smoke design

The buffer holds 2 directional and 2,000 point/spot lights in a 40 × 10 × 40 m volume, with ranges of 2–8 m and assorted cones. A compute probe (`GpuLightProbe.slang`) shades 512 sample points with random normals, views and materials, summing `StandardPbrEvaluateBrdf` × radiance over every light.

Each sample must match `Lights::ShadeAllLights` over the CPU mirror within 10⁻⁴ + 0.2 %, report the live light count, and more than half the samples must be lit.
