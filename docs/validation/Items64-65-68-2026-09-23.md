### Native smoke design

`ClusteredLightingMatchesTheCpuReference` fills a `GpuLightBuffer` with 2 directional and 1,500 point/spot lights (seed 164), then runs the assigner, a synthetic reverse-Z depth buffer with 16 sky columns, the heatmap and a 1,024-sample compute probe. It checks:

- **View lights:** within 10⁻⁴ relative of `CullLight`. A cull decision may only differ for a sphere within 10⁻³·(1 + r) of the volume.
- **Cluster AABBs:** within 10⁻⁴ relative of `ClusterBounds`.
- **Records and lists** against `AssignLights` over the GPU's own view lights and AABBs:
  - offsets are the exact prefix sum;
  - lists are sorted;
  - lights present in only one list graze the AABB within 10⁻³·(1 + r);
  - at most 0.5 % of clusters differ.
- **Stats:** equal to the CPU stats bit for bit when no list differs; the written/dropped identities always hold.
- **Probe:**
  - the brute-force sum is within 2·10⁻³ relative of `ShadeAllLights`;
  - clustered shading is within 10⁻³ of brute force where the list is complete, and never brighter where it was truncated.
- **Heatmap:** each channel within 1/255 of `HeatmapPixel` except at most 0.5 % of pixels (slice boundaries); sky transparent.

## Defects found

- **`ClusterGridRecord` size.** The first draft asserted 112 bytes. The real std430 size is 128; the assert and comments were corrected before any shader consumed it.
- **Dangling test references (GCC `-Wdangling-reference` and review).** Several `SWIM_CHECK_EQUAL` calls bound a member of a temporary (`ClusterBounds(...).Max[2]`, `map.at` on a temporary). They now compare by value.
- **Synthetic depth.** The smoke's first depth surface could reach negative view depths. It now stays within 1–52 m.
