# Phases 22 and 23 validation: the assembled runtime, September 25, 2026

This record covers Phase 22 (OpenGL and the editor removed; engine state, clock, tags and behaviours rebuilt) and Phase 23 (the modern renderer and UI assembled into `Swim Engine`, and the sandbox demo). The architecture is described in [Engine runtime](../EngineRuntime.md). The retired code is listed in [`Deprecated/README.md`](../../Deprecated/README.md).

## Environment

- Linux container, GCC 13, `linux-debug` preset, configured with `-DSDL_X11=OFF -DSDL_WAYLAND=OFF -DSDL_UNIX_CONSOLE_BUILD=ON` (no display server).
- GPU runs: SwiftShader (Vulkan 1.3, `VK_ICD_FILENAMES` pointing at its ICD) with SDL's offscreen video driver.
- No Khronos validation layer is available in this container, so GPU runs pass `--validation=off`. Lavapipe could not be installed.
- Physics: Jolt (PhysX is not built on Linux).

### New cases

- **`Engine.StateMachine` (5):** the single initial state and rejected masks; pause and resume only from the right state; listeners see every transition in order; listeners may unsubscribe while notified; names and tokens.
- **`Engine.SimulationClock` (5):** fixed steps accumulate real time; time scale slows and 0 freezes; pause stops time and single steps advance exactly one tick; long frames are clamped and excess steps dropped; domains follow simulation or real time.
- **`Engine.SceneRuntime` (9):** these cases run a real `SwimEngine` headless without a GPU (`--no-render`), with probe behaviours:
  - the lifecycle runs once and in order (Awake, Init, Update, FixedUpdate, Exit on engine exit);
  - pause freezes Playing-only behaviours, all-state behaviours get zero simulation time and wall-clock real time, and `step` / `step 3` advance exactly one or three ticks;
  - time scale halves the fixed steps but not real-time deltas;
  - Stop resets the scene (Exit + Init at the next frame, destroyed entities restored), and Play starts fresh;
  - engines may start paused (not reported as a transition);
  - scene switches are deferred to the next frame, and Awake runs once per instance;
  - the tag index and names stay consistent through add, remove, destroy and snapshot iteration;
  - the command buffer defers create, destroy and rename;
  - Jolt bodies fall and settle, collision callbacks fire, raycasts resolve to entities, and pause stops stepping.
- **`Engine.UiRuntime` (4):** bundled fonts and a missing folder; draw-list order (world canvases, then screen canvases by order) with hidden canvases skipped; canvases follow the scene and leave with it; constant-size billboards behind the camera are skipped rather than fatal.
- **`Engine.Camera` (1):** the runtime camera's projection equals `PerspectiveReverseZRowMajor`; near maps to depth 1 and farther points fall toward 0; the right-handed, +Y up, −Z forward view.
- **`Engine.FlyCamera` (3):** look by mouse delta times sensitivity (only while looking); movement along the view with world up, boost, cancellation and normalized diagonals; the wheel scales speed only while looking, within the limits.
- **`Engine.ProceduralMeshes` (5):** every shape is closed, wound outward and has a valid tangent space; documented bounds and counts; tangents follow +u; mesh assets carry one `StandardVertex` stream; skinned-column weights are normalized and follow height.
- **`Game.TentacleAnimator` (1):** the palette is a rigid chain of equal segments with a planted root, moving over time.
- **`Game.Sandbox` (7):** these cases run the whole sandbox headless:
  - every playground is built (28 gallery spheres, 576 hall instances, 6 skinned tentacles, 3 emitters, 15+ lights with exactly 3 shadowed, the HUD and world UI);
  - balls fall, rest and are counted, and their lifetimes remove them;
  - a paused world holds still, and Stop restores it;
  - commands work (sun, tabs, HUD, fire, views);
  - single steps work;
  - camera bookmarks work, including in every view and while paused;
  - startup `--exec` commands still apply after the scene's first frame.
- **`Game.UiBindings` (1):** handlers see changes but not the initial state.
- **`Game.Findings` (1):** every finding has a title, a detail and a known status.

## Defects found and fixed while assembling

The full list, with status, is in [the findings](../EngineRuntime.md#findings). These were found by the new tests or the captures:

- Behaviour `Exit` ran twice on scene exit.
- Cached component pointers could dangle.
- A single step did nothing for Playing-only behaviours.
- Stop did not reset the scene.
- Headless runs had no Vulkan loader.
- SwiftShader lacks `DrawParameters`, and its `vkCmdDrawIndexedIndirectCount` draws nothing (worked around).
- The push-constant stage masks were wrong.
- Timings were read before the first frame.
- The sky constants did not fit 128 bytes.
- Constant-size billboards behind the camera threw and ended the run (found by the fountain capture).
- UI bindings reported initial values as changes, which reset the camera view after a startup `--exec` (found by the PBR capture).

## Headless captures

Each capture is 1280×720 at 30 frames with `--fixed-delta=0.033`. It runs from `build/linux-debug`:

```sh
SDL_VIDEO_DRIVER=offscreen VK_ICD_FILENAMES=<swiftshader>/vk_swiftshader_icd.json \
"./Swim Engine" --headless --size=1280x720 --frames=30 --fixed-delta=0.033 --validation=off \
  --exec="sandbox.view 1" --exec="sandbox.tab 1" --capture=pbr.ppm
```

| --- | --- | --- |

| [Fountain](images/Phase22-23-fountain.jpg) | `sandbox.view 4`, `sandbox.hud 0` | GPU particles (fountain, smoke, sparks) and GPU-skinned tentacles, without the HUD |
| [Physics](images/Phase22-23-physics.jpg) | `sandbox.view 3`, `sandbox.drop 25` | The physics playground with dropped balls, shadows from the sun and spot light |

The diagnostics overlay in these runs reports:

- CPU frame time of about 11–13 ms;
- GPU time of about 3.3–3.8 s on SwiftShader across 68 passes (the costliest are Forward+ opaque, the post histogram, the TAA resolve and GTAO);
- 659–684 GPU objects, 1 directional and 14 local lights, and 10 shadow views from 3 casters;
- 3 emitters and 6 skinned instances;
- about 900 UI quads.

These are software-rasterizer numbers and are not budgets. The 1080p desktop budgets are still to be recorded.
